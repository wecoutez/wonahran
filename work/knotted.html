Knotted Gwangju Sangmu

Knotted’s move into a new region, designed as a playful garden rather than a copy of the Seoul stores.

Client
GFFG
Location
Gwangju, Korea
Year
2024
Role
Creative Director
Scope
Creative direction, spatial storytelling, VMD, artist commission

Opening in Gwangju Sangmu meant answering a question every expanding brand runs into: how much of the original do you keep? I directed the store toward brand consistency in tone — soft, inviting, generous — while letting the material palette feel local and new.

We introduced an experimental tile selection and custom lighting structures to read the room as a garden, and commissioned a mural from the artist Isulro depicting a whimsical garden scene. Character-led product zones give both loyal fans and first-time visitors a reason to linger.
