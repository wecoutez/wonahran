Knotted Lifestyle Expansion

A proposal for taking Knotted out of food entirely — collaborations, apparel, living goods, pet products.

Client
GFFG
Location
Seoul, Korea
Year
2024
Role
Creative Director
Scope
Brand extension strategy, collaboration targeting, merchandise line design

The strategic question was whether a dessert brand could hold attention in categories it had never entered. The proposal maps collaboration targets across industries — Porsche, BTS, Musinsa, AOMG, Kia, Samsung — alongside a full merchandise line spanning fashion, living and pet.

Read together, it positions Knotted as a culturally relevant lifestyle brand with touchpoints in a customer’s day that have nothing to do with eating.
