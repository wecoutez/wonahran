Disney D100 — Hyundai Pangyo

Disney’s centenary pop-up in a Hyundai department store, from first massing model to shop drawings.

Client
The Walt Disney Company
Location
Pangyo, Korea
Year
2023
Role
Creative Manager, APAC
Scope
Space planning, Rhino 3D modelling, experiential zone design, shop drawings, SD deck

The D100 anniversary needed a Korean expression that could survive the constraints of a department store floorplate. I handled initial space planning and 3D model building in Rhino, designed the experiential zone, and produced shop drawings for the main entry and furniture.

I also compiled the schematic design deck and managed local communication between the global brand team and Korean fabricators — the part of the job where a design either survives or quietly dies.
