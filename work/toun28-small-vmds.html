Toun28 Retail VMD Stands

A modular stand system deployed across four retail environments with different footprints and rules.

Client
Toun28
Location

Year
2019–2020
Role
Art Director
Scope
Creation and production of VMD stands and retail goods across four locations

Stands for Aritaum Gangnam, Starfield Hanam and Thingool Yeouido — three host retailers, three sets of constraints, one brand that had to look like itself in all of them.

The work covered both the stands and the retail-related goods that populated them.
