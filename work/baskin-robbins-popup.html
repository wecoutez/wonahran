Baskin-Robbins Korea

Baskin-Robbins Seongsu Pop-Up

A gacha-shop pop-up built around a seasonal mango flavour, designed to pull a younger audience back into the brand.

Client
Baskin-Robbins Korea
Location
Seongsu, Seoul
Year
2025
Role
Creative Marketing Team Lead
Scope
Concept direction, spatial design, IP integration, campaign rollout

Baskin-Robbins Korea faces a customer base that has been quietly ageing. The Seongsu pop-up was an argument that the brand still belongs to people in their teens and twenties — a bright, high-saturation retail moment sited in the neighbourhood where that audience already spends its weekends.

The space borrows the logic of a gacha machine: arrive, play, leave with something. Product discovery is staged as a game rather than a menu board, and every surface is built to be photographed.
