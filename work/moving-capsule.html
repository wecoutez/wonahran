Lexus Design Award

Moving Capsule

“What you own is not who you are.” Prototype winner, 2017 Lexus Design Award.

Client
Lexus Design Award
Location
La Triennale di Milano
Year
2017
Role
Designer
Scope
Concept, prototype design and fabrication

Furniture that packs into its own container — a proposal for living lightly, made for people whose homes are temporary by circumstance rather than choice.

Selected as a Prototype Winner of the 2017 Lexus Design Award under a jury including Paola Antonelli and Toyo Ito, and exhibited at La Triennale di Milano.
