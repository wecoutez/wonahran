Baskin-Robbins Korea

Bonjour! Paris — Robin

An original character built for a single month of ice cream — and then given a film, a walkable city and a product line to live in.

Client
Baskin-Robbins Korea
Location
Seoul, Korea
Year
2025
Role
Creative Marketing Team Lead
Scope
Original character development, story and storyboard, film direction and edit, experiential event design, merchandise line direction

Baskin-Robbins Korea launches a Flavor of the Month every month, which is a lot of launches and very few of them are remembered. The usual sequence is to settle the flavour and then decorate it. November 2025 ran the other way: we built a character first, gave him a day, and let the flavour arrive inside his story.

Robin is ours. I developed him with the illustrator Enrique — a boy in a red cap and a striped shirt, a friend with round glasses, and a small white terrier, drawn in the flat, warm line of mid-century European children’s books. The look was chosen for a reason: it reads as something a customer already half-remembers, which buys affection faster than a new mascot normally earns it.

I wrote the story and boarded it, then cut the film in Premiere. A Fun Day with My Buddy follows Robin through one day in Paris — a balloon from a street performer, an ice cream truck on the Champs-Élysées, a park full of geese, an accidental appearance in a tourist couple’s photograph, and the sun going down over the Seine. Six panels, fifty-six seconds, no dialogue.

The trade launch turned the story into a room. Bonjour Town ran as a six-zone walkthrough at WORKSHOP on 28 October: guests entered on a boarding pass, walked into Paris through sound and scent, sat among the characters, met the flavour at an ice cream truck, photo-bombed a Parisian street scene, and left through balloons with a five-cut strip in hand. Staff were costumed into the illustration.

The merchandise line put the same drawing onto things people keep — totes, tumblers, plush, striped pyjamas, notebooks, luggage tags, picnic blankets. A character invented for one month is now an asset the brand owns outright, and can bring back.

Character illustration with Enrique. Story, storyboard and film edit by Ahran Won. Merchandise images are concept renderings from the proposal stage.
