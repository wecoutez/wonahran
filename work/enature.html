IT’S HANBUL

enature

Rebuilding an established natural cosmetics brand’s visual language from the identity down.

Client
IT’S HANBUL
Location
Seoul, Korea
Year
2020–2022
Role
Creative Director
Scope
Brand identity overhaul, visual language, packaging design, photoshoot direction

enature was already a leading natural cosmetics brand with a real commitment to sustainability — the problem was that its visual language no longer said so. The work was a strategic overhaul of the identity, including a distinctive new visual system and product packaging designed to echo the brand’s ecological position.

I directed the conceptual photoshoots that carried the new narrative, tuning the visual story to the brand’s philosophy rather than to category convention.
