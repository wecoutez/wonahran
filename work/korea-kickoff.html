The Walt Disney Company

Disney CPGP Korea Kickoff

A licensee showcase designed, bid and built in three weeks on a constrained budget.

Client
The Walt Disney Company
Location
Seoul, Korea
Year
2022
Role
Creative Manager, APAC
Scope
SD, DD, agency bidding, on-site supervision

The kickoff event had to present Disney’s upcoming product slate to potential licensees — an audience that decides where to place bets — with three weeks of runway and a budget that ruled out most of the obvious moves.

We planned it as a sequence of high-impact set pieces rather than an evenly finished room, spending the budget where buyers would actually stand.
