Knotted Guui

Bakery, café and lifestyle retail folded into a single environment.

Client
GFFG
Location
Guui, Seoul
Year
2024
Role
Creative Director
Scope
Store concept, retail zoning, merchandise display integration

Guui was where Knotted stopped being only a bakery. The store combines a clear bakery counter, seated café space and merchandise display in one plan, using the signature pastel green palette and warm wood finishes to hold the three programmes together.

Zoning does the strategic work: a customer can come for a donut and leave having handled the brand’s products, which is the whole argument for Knotted as a lifestyle brand rather than a dessert shop.
