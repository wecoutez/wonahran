About

Between brand
and building.

I am a creative director working between brand and building. Most of my career has been spent on the problem of what happens when a brand stops being a logo and has to become a room — a store, a pop-up, a shelf a customer reaches toward.

That interest came out of training. I studied architecture at the School of the Art Institute of Chicago and landscape architecture at the Harvard Graduate School of Design, and spent my first years in practice in the United States on landscape and architecture projects. When I moved into brand work, I kept the discipline: schematic design, design development, construction documents, bidding, on-site supervision. It is an unusual vocabulary for a brand director, and it is the reason my work gets built the way it was drawn.

Since then I have created a brand from nothing and taken it to launch, rebuilt an established one, run retail design across the Asia-Pacific region for The Walt Disney Company, led a three-team creative division through more than twenty-five venue builds, and directed the creative marketing team at Baskin-Robbins Korea. The scale changes. The question does not.
